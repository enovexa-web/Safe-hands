Care We Provide

Our Services

01

Home Care Nursing Services

Qualified nurses provide professional home nursing care around the clock, with flexible 24-hour and 12-hour services to suit every family. From elderly care, bedridden patient care and post-operative nursing to wound dressing and wound management, injections and IV medication, catheter care and regular vital-sign monitoring, our nurses also help with personal hygiene and daily living, and offer compassionate palliative and long-term care support, so your loved one stays comfortable, safe and well looked after at home.

02

Allopathy Doctor Consultation

Modern Medical Care at Your Doorstep

Experienced doctors bring modern medical care to your doorstep, with general physician consultations and home visits that spare elderly patients the strain of travelling to a clinic. Our doctors help with diabetes and blood pressure monitoring, fever and common illnesses, follow-up consultations and clear medication guidance and review, so every patient receives timely, trustworthy care in the comfort of home.

03

Ayurvedic Doctor Consultation

Traditional Healing for Holistic Wellness

Qualified Ayurvedic doctors offer personalised treatment guidance rooted in traditional healing, including consultations for joint pain and arthritis and the long-term management of chronic health conditions. Guidance also covers lifestyle and wellness, Ayurvedic diet recommendations and the Ayurvedic therapies best suited to each person, so everyone is supported towards balanced, holistic wellness.

04

Patient Care Services

Attentive day-to-day care for patients with ongoing health needs, including personal hygiene, feeding support, comfort and close observation of their condition. Our team works alongside doctors, reports any changes promptly and keeps families informed at every step, so the patient is always safe, comfortable and well looked after.

05

Elderly Care Services

Respectful, personalised care for senior citizens that protects independence and dignity. We help with daily routines, serve nutritious meals, encourage gentle activity and offer genuine companionship in a warm, homely setting, so every elder feels valued, safe and truly at home.

06

Home Care Assistance

Practical help with everyday tasks such as bathing, dressing, grooming, toileting, light housekeeping and meal preparation. Our caregivers also assist with medicines on time and accompany elders on short outings or appointments, so they can continue to live comfortably and confidently in familiar surroundings.

07

Baby Care Services

Gentle, trained support for newborns and infants, covering feeding, bathing, massage, soothing, sleep routines and hygiene care. Our caregivers follow safe, hygienic practices and guide new parents with patience and kindness, so mothers can rest and recover with complete peace of mind.

08

Post-Hospitalization Care

Careful support after hospital discharge to help patients recover safely at home. This includes medication reminders, dressing and wound care, help with walking and mobility, a healthy diet routine and close watch for warning signs, with the doctor contacted quickly whenever needed, so recovery is smooth and the risk of readmission is reduced.

09

Bedridden Patient Care

Compassionate care for patients who cannot leave their bed, including regular repositioning, bed baths, pressure-sore prevention, feeding, oral care, toileting and hygiene. Every task is carried out gently and always with dignity, keeping the patient clean, comfortable and cared for, and giving the family reassurance and rest.

10

Trained Caregiver Services

Skilled caregivers trained in elder and patient care who bring patience, kindness and practical know-how to every shift. Whether you need a few hours, day or night duty, or full-time live-in support, our caregivers are reliable, well-mannered and ready to adapt to the specific needs of your loved one.

11

Physiotherapy Support

Guided exercises and therapy to improve mobility, strength and balance, ease pain and speed recovery after illness, surgery, stroke or injury. Sessions are planned around each person’s condition and carried out at a comfortable pace, with progress tracked and family members shown simple exercises to continue at home.

12

Palliative Care Services

Gentle, comfort-focused care for people living with serious or long-term illness, easing pain and distress while giving patients and families emotional support. We focus on quality of life, from symptom relief and nutrition to calm companionship, so every day is lived with comfort, respect and dignity.

Not sure which service is right for your loved one? Talk to our team and we’ll help you choose.
